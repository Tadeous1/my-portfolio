READOUT

One market dominates the source

The United Kingdom contributes £9.03m of the £10.67m completed-line revenue in this calculation, with smaller international markets following behind.

This is a public-data demonstration and not a claim that the original portfolio dashboard used the UCI extract.

METHOD & LIMITATIONS

How this dashboard was built

Source. UCI Online Retail dataset, 541,909 raw line-item records covering December 2010 to December 2011.

Processing. Excluded cancellation invoices and non-positive quantities or prices, then calculated revenue as quantity × unit price and grouped by month, country, and product description.

Limitations. This page is descriptive only. It does not claim forecasting accuracy, profitability, or causal explanations for the sales patterns.
