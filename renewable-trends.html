METHOD & LIMITATIONS

How this dashboard was built

Source. Our World in Data’s renewable-energy-generation grapher, whose chart cites the Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy (2026).

Processing. Selected the World entity, retained solar, wind, and hydropower series, and prepared checkpoint and latest-year summaries.

Limitations. This page presents a world-level descriptive view. It does not forecast future generation or explain causal drivers.
